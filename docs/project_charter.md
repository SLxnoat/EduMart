# EduMart Project Charter

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** EduMart  
**Project Description:** An online learning material store - a web-based e-commerce platform for buying and selling study materials including past papers, e-books, model papers, revision notes, and recorded lecture packs.  
**Problem Statement:** Study materials are currently scattered across Facebook groups, WhatsApp, and photocopy shops, making it difficult to find good material with no trusted platform for secure payment.  
**Solution:** EduMart provides one trusted marketplace connecting students, tutors, and institutes to buy, sell, and access learning materials in one place.

## Project Objectives
1. Build a centralized marketplace for learning materials
2. Support both digital and physical delivery methods
3. Empower sellers to manage their own content
4. Maintain high quality through reviews and admin approval
5. Enable quick discovery of materials through search and filtering
6. Ensure safe and reliable transactions through secure payment processing
7. Provide 24/7 customer support through AI chatbot
8. Enable promotional campaigns through coupon and discount management
9. Facilitate seller management with dedicated dashboard

## Project Scope
**In Scope:**
- User authentication and profile management (Student / Tutor / Admin roles)
- Material catalog management with categories & subjects
- Search and filtering capabilities by subject, grade, exam year, price
- Shopping cart and multi-item checkout functionality
- Secure payment gateway integration
- Order management and purchase history system
- Digital delivery - instant download links for e-materials
- Physical delivery tracking for printed materials
- Admin dashboard for oversight - approve listings, manage users & orders
- Review and rating system for each material
- Notification system - email/SMS order & upload alerts
- Seller (Tutor) Dashboard - uploads, sales & earnings
- AI Chatbot - 24/7 instant help, FAQs & order status queries
- Coupon & Discount Management for promotions
- Responsive web application (desktop, tablet, mobile)

**Out of Scope:**
- Mobile native applications (iOS/Android)
- Integration with external educational platforms
- Advanced analytics dashboard (beyond basic admin dashboard)
- Multi-currency support (initial launch (shipping logistics integration
